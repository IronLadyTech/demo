Power Pitch
Pitching · 3 min
The 60-Second
Power Pitch
Own your story. Speak it with authority.
01
Who you are
Say aloud: "I am [Name], [Title] at [Company]. I specialise in [expertise]." Stand tall. No apology in your voice.
02
What you do differently
Say aloud: "What makes my approach different is [your unique method or result]." Be specific. Numbers win.
03
The hook
End with: "Right now I am working on [project/goal] and it is [result/impact]." Make them want to ask more.
04
The repeat drill
Say the full pitch three times — faster each round. The goal is fluency without thinking. Your confidence lives in your repetition.

Leadership Mirror
Self-Awareness · 5 min
The Leadership
Mirror
Honest answers build powerful leaders.
Q1
Where did you lead today?
Describe one moment today where you made a decision, influenced someone, or moved something forward. Write it down.
Q2
Where did you hold back?
One moment where you played small — stayed quiet, deferred, or apologised unnecessarily. Name it.
Q3
What will you do differently tomorrow?
One specific action. Not a goal — an action. Something you will actually do in the next 24 hours.

Own the Silence
Communication · 4 min
Own the
Silence
Whoever speaks first after a number, loses.
01
Set your number
Write the number you want — salary, fee, budget. Not the safe number. The real one.
02
Say it out loud
Speak it clearly. No hedging. No "I was thinking maybe around…". Just the number. Then — stop talking. Practice now.
03
Hold the silence — 10 seconds
After you say your number, start the timer. Do not speak. Do not explain. Authority lives in stillness.

Journal
Daily reflection is your competitive advantage.

Power Pose Reset
Confidence · 2 min
The Power
Pose Reset
Claim your space before you enter the room.
01
Ground yourself
Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Feel the floor. You are not a guest here. Take a slow, full breath in through your nose.
02
Expand your space
Shoulders back. Chin level. Eyes forward. Place your hands on your hips or open your arms wide. Hold this for 60 seconds — your body is telling your brain who you are.
03
Your power statement
Say aloud or in your mind: "I am prepared. I belong here. I lead." Say it three times. Believe the third one.
